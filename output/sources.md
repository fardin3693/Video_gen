# Scientific sources and claim audit

Research/access date: **2026-11-26**. These sources support the physics, not the literal disappearance of a star. The scenario is expressly impossible under ordinary conservation of mass-energy. It is not an explosion, an eclipse, or the Sun becoming a black hole.

## References

1. **All About the Sun — NASA Space Place**. Publication date not clearly established in the fetched result; accessed 2026-11-26. https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/all-about-the-sun/en/
   - Sunlight takes approximately eight minutes and twenty seconds to reach Earth. This is rounded; the Earth–Sun distance changes during the year.
2. **More Quick Facts — NASA Astrobiology**. Publication date not specified in the consulted result. https://astrobiology.nasa.gov/quick-facts/more-quick-facts/
   - Independently restates the 8:20 light travel time, and the Sun's role in photosynthesis and warmth.
3. **Speed of Gravity and Light? — University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, Physics Van**. Initial answer published 2010-09-14; follow-ups 2011–2017. https://van.physics.illinois.edu/ask/listing/16045
   - Changes in gravity cannot arrive instantaneously; approximately eight-minute propagation delay. Explicitly explains that disappearance itself violates the physical rules and that an explosion does not remove mass-energy.
4. **Climate and Earth’s Energy Budget — NASA Earth Observatory / NASA Science**. Publication date not reliably established from the fetched page; accessed 2026-11-26. https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/climate-and-earths-energy-budget/
   - Solar input warms the Earth system and drives photosynthesis; Earth emits thermal infrared radiation. Removing the input produces a cooling imbalance. Ocean/atmosphere thermal storage prevents instantaneous freezing.
5. **Global Warming — NASA Science, Earth Observatory**. Publication date not specified here. https://science.nasa.gov/earth/climate-change/global-warming/
   - Absorption of solar energy heats land and ocean; greenhouse absorption and reradiation help govern surface temperature. Used as a corroborating energy-balance source, not a quantitative sunless-Earth climate model.
6. **The Depths Below — Life on a Vent — NOAA Ocean Today**. Page date unspecified; transcript describes the 2011 Okeanos Explorer expedition. https://oceantoday.noaa.gov/lifeonavent/
   - Hydrothermal vents and chemosynthetic bacteria/archaea; chemical energy rather than sunlight supports the base of these food webs.
7. **NASA Study Reproduces Origins of Life on Ocean Floor — NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory**. Published 2019-02-25; updated 2019-02-26. https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasa-study-reproduces-origins-of-life-on-ocean-floor/
   - Chemical energy in hydrothermal settings and the possibility of habitable environments far from sunlight. This study reproduced chemical precursors, **not living organisms**; the film does not claim otherwise.
8. **Tapping the Earth’s Natural Heat — USGS Circular 1125**, Wendell A. Duffield, J. H. Sass, M. L. Sorey. Published 1994. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3133/cir1125 . Official record: https://www.usgs.gov/publications/tapping-earths-natural-heat
   - Earth's interior produces heat, particularly through radioactive decay, independently of incoming sunlight.
9. **Some Unanswered Questions — This Dynamic Earth, USGS**. Page publication date not established here. https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/dynamic/unanswered.html
   - Internal heat from residual formation energy and radioactive decay. Used only for these heat sources; unrelated historical statements elsewhere on the page are not adopted.

## Claim-by-claim decisions

| Spoken claim | Basis | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Nothing noticeable for about 8:20 | 1, 2, 3 | Rounded average travel time, not an exact universal countdown |
| Light already in flight still arrives | 1–3 | Causal propagation; no instantaneous change at Earth |
| Gravity changes travel at light speed | 3 | Applies to propagation of changes, not a Newtonian delayed force aimed at the Sun's old position |
| Earth leaves its solar orbit along a tangent | 3 + inertia | Idealized removal of the Sun; other bodies still exert gravity, hence “roughly” |
| No instant freeze; ongoing surface cooling | 4, 5 | Qualitative energy-balance inference; no invented daily temperature milestones |
| Photosynthesis stops without sunlight | 2, 4 | Refers to naturally sunlit photosynthesis, not organisms artificially illuminated by humans |
| Some microbes can use chemical energy | 6, 7 | No claim that all vent animals are independent of photosynthetic oxygen |
| Internal heat persists | 8, 9 | Not a promise of eternal geothermal heat or indefinite human survival |
| Some life could persist | 6–9, explicitly conditional inference | No survival duration, population, or guarantee asserted |
