# Title
What If the Sun Vanished? We'd Get 8 More Minutes

# YouTube description
The Sun disappears. For about eight minutes and twenty seconds, Earth would seem unchanged. Then comes darkness—but not instant freezing, and perhaps not the end of every living thing.

This is an impossible thought experiment, not a prediction: stars cannot simply vanish under known physics. We use it to explore light-travel time, gravity, Earth's stored heat, and microbes powered by chemistry. The long-term frozen landscape is an illustration, not a timed climate forecast. Some vent animals still depend on photosynthetically produced oxygen; the survival discussion concerns some microbes, not all ocean life.

AI-generated scientific illustrations and a synthetic English narration are used. Diagrams are simplified and not to scale.
